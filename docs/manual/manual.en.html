Live Transcriber

User manual

Version dev ·

Live Transcriber turns Romanian speech into text while people talk. Put the computer on the table, press Start, and read along. Everything runs on your own computer: no account, no internet after the first start, and the audio never leaves the machine.

1Install and first start

  1. Download the installer from github.com/Denis-Feier/local-word-transcriptor/releases, under Assets:
    • Mac with an Apple processor (M1 or newer), macOS 12 or newer: the file ending in -mac-arm64.dmg
    • Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit: the file ending in -windows-setup.exe
  2. Install. Mac: open the .dmg and drag Live Transcriber into Applications. Windows: run the installer.
  3. Open it the first time. The app is not digitally signed yet, so the system warns once. Mac: open the app, close the message, then go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway (on macOS 14 or older, right-click the app → Open). Windows: at “Windows protected your PC” click More info, then Run anyway.
  4. Allow the microphone when the Mac asks.
  5. Wait for the model. The first time, the app downloads the Romanian speech model (1.1 GB) and shows the progress at the top right. After that it works without internet.

2Transcribing a conversation

Press Start and talk. Grey text with a blinking cursor is the live guess while someone speaks. After a short pause (about 0.7 s) the sentence is written again in full and turns black: that is the final text, and it is saved straight away. Press Stop when you are done.

  1. Start / Stop: starts and stops listening.
  2. Level meter: moves with the sound the mic hears and says speech while someone talks. If it barely moves, the mic is too far away.
  3. Projector: large captions for a projector or TV (section 5).
  4. Settings: speech detection and the projector display (section 6).
  5. Copy, Download .txt, Clear: copy the text, save a copy with times, or empty the screen and start a new transcript file.
  6. Model: the speech model. Keep the default (section 8).
  7. Mic 1, Mic 2: which microphones listen. System default is the computer's own. A second person can get a second mic (section 3).
  8. Speaker: plays the voices on a speaker in the room (section 4). None is off.
  9. Noise suppression: filters steady noise such as air conditioning or traffic. Off by default, because it can also swallow quiet, distant voices.
  10. Save audio: also keeps the recording as a sound file next to the transcript (about 115 MB per hour and mic).
  11. Status: the model in use and ready; on the first start, the download progress.

Transcripts are saved in Documents → Live Transcriber (menu File → Open transcripts folder). Stop and Start continue the same file; Clear starts a new one. Your choice of microphones and model is remembered for next time.

3Two people, two microphones

For an interview, a doctor and a patient, or a panel of two, give each person their own microphone, close to them, and choose the second one under Mic 2. Any microphone the computer sees works: built-in, USB, jack, Bluetooth, or the receiver of a wireless kit.

  • Every line says who heard it: Mic 1 blue, Mic 2 orange, also in the projector view and as Mic 1: / Mic 2: in the saved file. Lines spoken at the same time are marked ⇄.
  • Each mic also hears the other person, only more quietly. The app keeps those words only under the mic that heard them best. If they still show up under the wrong mic, see Mic separation in section 7.
  • A mic that disconnects (Bluetooth out of range, flat battery, cable) shows not connected. The other mic keeps recording, and the first one joins again by itself once it is back.

4Playing the voices on a speaker

If the audience needs to hear the people talking, connect a speaker and choose it under Speaker. It plays straight away, even before Start, so you can do a sound check: talk and watch the meter. Start and Stop don't affect it; None turns it off. The app always opens with None, so it never starts howling by surprise. Volume is remembered.

  • Prefer a cable or USB speaker. Bluetooth speakers play about 0.2 s late, and the person talking hears that as an echo.
  • Howling means a microphone hears the speaker. Put the speaker in front of the mics, facing away from them towards the audience, and turn Volume down. A laptop's own speakers almost always howl with its built-in mic.
  • With two mics, keep the speaker away from both, or some lines may appear under the wrong mic.
  • A Bluetooth speaker with its own microphone (many karaoke speakers): choose your real mics under Mic 1 and Mic 2 instead of System default, or the computer may record from the speaker's microphone and both the sound and the text get worse.
  • A speaker that disconnects shows not connected and goes silent; the sound does not jump to the laptop. It plays again once it is back.

5Captions on a projector or TV

Press Projector (or P): the window fills with large captions, the newest sentence brightest. Move the mouse to show the controls; they hide again after a few seconds, so the audience sees only text. The light in the bottom-left corner shows the microphone: grey off, green listening, orange someone speaking.

Projector as a second screen (not mirrored): open Settings → Open caption window. It opens on the projector, full screen; if it doesn't, drag it there and press F. It shows only the captions, while your window keeps all the controls out of the audience's sight. Text size, Sentences shown and Colours are also in Settings, under Projector display.

6Settings for speech detection

The defaults suit most rooms. If something goes wrong, change one setting at a time while someone is talking, and watch the text. Changes apply immediately and are remembered. A dot ● marks a changed setting; Reset to defaults undoes them all.

SettingWhat it does
Speech detection threshold
default 0.50		 How sure the app must be that a sound is speech. Lower catches quiet or distant voices; higher ignores background noise.
Pause that ends a sentence
default 0.7 s		 The silence that closes a sentence. Longer suits people who speak slowly or pause mid-sentence.
Ignore sounds shorter than
default 250 ms		 Drops coughs, clicks and moving chairs.
Longest sentence
default 20 s		 Longer speech is split at its quietest moment.
Live text refresh
default 0.5 s		 How often the grey live text updates.
Mic separation
default 8 dB		 Only with two mics. Lower ignores more of the other person; higher keeps both when people talk at the same time.

7Adjusting for different speakers and rooms

Find what you see, make the change, and check it while the person talks.

What you seeWhat to do
A quiet, soft-spoken or distant person is missed; the meter barely moves Move the mic closer or give them their own. Then lower Speech detection threshold to 0.35–0.40.
Background noise (air conditioning, traffic, chatter) turns into stray words Raise Speech detection threshold to 0.6–0.7. For steady noise, also tick Noise suppression.
A slow speaker, or one who stops to think, gets sentences cut in two Pause that ends a sentence: 1.2–2 s.
A fast speaker who never pauses fills the screen with one huge line Longest sentence: 10–15 s.
Quick back-and-forth on one mic: two people's words end up in one line Pause that ends a sentence: 0.5 s, or one mic per person.
Coughs, clicks or moving chairs turn into words Ignore sounds shorter than: 300–400 ms.
Very short answers (“Da.”, “Nu.”) are lost Ignore sounds shorter than: 150 ms.
Two mics: one person's words also appear under the other mic Mic separation: 5 dB, and move the mics further apart.
Two mics: when both talk at once, one of them is lost Mic separation: 12 dB.
A presenter who walks around A wireless clip-on mic as Mic 1.
A group around a table The laptop in the middle, screen up. For a long table, a USB conference mic.
Text appears late on an older computer Model: Parakeet Romanian 110M (section 8), and Live text refresh: 1 s.
Strong accents, dialect, mumbling No setting fixes these; a microphone close to the mouth helps most.

8Choosing a model

Keep NVIDIA Canary 1B v2, the default and the most accurate. If text appears slowly on an older computer, choose Parakeet Romanian 110M: it is much faster, but writes in lowercase without punctuation. The other models in the list are alternatives to try. Models not downloaded yet show their size; choosing one asks first, downloads it and switches to it when it is ready. The choice is remembered.

9When something goes wrong

ProblemWhat to do
Start does nothing, or “Microphone access was blocked” Mac: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone, turn on Live Transcriber. Windows: Settings → Privacy & security → Microphone, allow desktop apps. Then restart the app.
A mic or the speaker shows not connected Plug it in, switch it on or pair it again. It joins again by itself.
The meter moves but no text appears Check that the right mic is chosen; lower Speech detection threshold.
The speaker howlsSee section 4.
Anything else Menu Help → Open logs folder, and send the file server.log with a short description on the project's GitHub page (Issues).
